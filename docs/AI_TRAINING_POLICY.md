# Machine-learning training and text/data mining policy

The project does **not** consent to bulk scraping of repositories, issues,
private artifacts or community communications for training generative models.
Where applicable law recognizes machine-readable reservations of rights for
text and data mining, the rightsholder expressly reserves those rights.

This policy does not secretly add a field-of-use restriction to files released
under an OSI-approved license. The AGPL remains the complete software license
for the engine, and its permissions cannot honestly be described as a blanket
legal prohibition on machine-learning training. Private and unpublished
materials receive no public license.

The project asks dataset builders and model developers to obtain affirmative
permission, document provenance, honor removal requests, identify the exact
release used, and preserve the creator's attribution.

A stronger enforceable “no AI training” term would require a non-open-source
license and specialist legal drafting. The project will not mislabel such a
restriction as free software.
