# Trademark policy

“ProofRay”, “ProofRay Intelligence” and the project's future logos identify the
canonical project created by Kaue Oliveira Costa (kyuubyN). “Horizon” identifies
the historical architecture and research lineage. Software licenses do not grant
trademark rights.

You may truthfully say that software is compatible with, based on, or derived
from ProofRay. You may not use the name or visual identity in a way that
suggests sponsorship, certification, authorship by, or official status from the
canonical project when none exists.

Unmodified redistribution may retain the original name when it clearly states
the distributor and preserves all notices. Modified distributions should use a
distinct product name and may state “based on ProofRay.” Historical and technical
references to the Horizon architecture are permitted when they do not imply that a
fork is the canonical ProofRay distribution.

This policy records project intent and is not a claim that a trademark has
already been registered in every jurisdiction.
