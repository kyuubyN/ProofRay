# Founder License and Compensation Agreement

This is plain-language project policy, not a substitute for legal advice —
before relying on it for a real dispute, have it reviewed by counsel in your
own jurisdiction, the same caveat this repository already gives for
`LICENSE_POLICY.md`, `RESPONSIBLE_USE.md` and `CLA.md`.

This document governs contributions from the project's co-founders — distinct
from `CLA.md` (external contributors) and `GOVERNANCE.md` (decision-making
authority). A co-founder's contribution is both larger and harder to separate
from the project than an external patch, so the license grant and the
compensation tied to it are made explicit here instead of left to informal
understanding.

## 1. License grant

Each co-founder grants the same license described in `CLA.md`'s "What you
grant" section — a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
irrevocable copyright and patent license under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, under
`Apache-2.0` for files that carry that dual-license header, and under any
future commercial license the project offers — over their own contributions,
identified by Git commit authorship in this repository.

This grant is made **in exchange for** the compensation terms in §2. It is not
a free exemption: a co-founder's contribution is licensed on the same
substantive terms as an external contributor's under `CLA.md`, just paired
with the compensation this document defines instead of being granted for
nothing.

## 2. Compensation on commercial exploitation

### 2.1 Trigger

A "Triggering Event" is any of:
- a paid commercial license sold to a third party;
- a sale or majority-equity transfer of the entity operating the project;
- a revenue-generating hosted/SaaS offering built on the project that exceeds
  **[ placeholder: e.g. R$ X gross/month ]** for two consecutive months.

### 2.2 Mandatory negotiation

Within **[ placeholder: e.g. 30 days ]** of a Triggering Event, all three
parties must negotiate in good faith the compensation percentage each
co-founder receives from the revenue or proceeds of that event, taking into
account at minimum:

- verifiable contribution volume at the time of the event (commits, lines,
  and documented non-code work) since the project's inception;
- the time each party has been actively contributing;
- the pre-existing work Kaue Oliveira Costa contributed before the other
  co-founders joined (2026-08-13 through each co-founder's respective join
  date), recognized as a standing baseline credit in his favor in every
  negotiation under this clause.

### 2.3 Deadlock fallback

If the three parties do not reach written agreement within the deadline in
§2.2, the split defaults automatically to:

- Kaue Oliveira Costa: **[ placeholder: e.g. 50% ]**, as the pre-existing-work
  baseline credit described in §2.2;
- the remaining **[ placeholder: e.g. 50% ]** divided among all co-founders
  (including Kaue, for his contribution after the baseline period) in
  proportion to verifiable commit/line contribution in this repository as of
  the Triggering Event date.

This default applies automatically and requires no further action from any
party.

### 2.4 No blocking

No party may unreasonably withhold agreement past the §2.2 deadline solely to
force renegotiation of §2.3's default. Doing so forfeits that party's right to
contest the §2.3 outcome.

## 3. What this does not do

Consistent with `GOVERNANCE.md`: this agreement does not grant voting power,
governance rights, maintainership, or change the founding role described
there. It is a license-for-compensation exchange, not a path to control.

## 4. Verification

Contribution share is evidenced by Git commit history and authorship, per
account, in this repository — a public, immutable record all three parties
accept as the basis for §2 unless a specific commit's authorship is disputed
in writing.
