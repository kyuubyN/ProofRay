# Governance

ProofRay is an open technical commons with a clearly preserved origin. It is
maintained under the institutional name ProofRay Intelligence and descends from
the Horizon research line in Q-HDRE.

## Founding role

Kaue Oliveira Costa (kyuubyN) is the creator, research lead and founding
maintainer. The founding role is historical and cannot be reassigned by a vote
or contribution. This holds regardless of `CLA.md` — signing it is a license
grant to the project, never a step toward governance rights.

## Technical decisions

Before a wider maintainer council exists, the founding maintainer accepts
releases, appoints maintainers, protects compatibility and resolves disputes.
Decisions should be justified by reproducible evidence, explicit scope and
honest limits. Benchmark labels never override a verified causal contradiction
without disclosure.

## Future delegation

Maintainer authority may be delegated publicly. A future foundation or council
must preserve:

- free access to the canonical implementation;
- provenance and verifiability as non-negotiable design principles;
- founding authorship and complete contributor attribution;
- transparent release and security processes;
- no retroactive removal of rights already granted.

Forks are permitted by the software license but may not imply official status.
